# Anti-Patterns (What NOT to do)

Any post containing these anti-patterns must be flagged or revised.

## Banned Openers
- ❌ "In today's fast-paced digital world..."
- ❌ "I'm thrilled/humbled/excited to announce..."
- ❌ "Let's dive in!" / "Here's a breakdown:"
- ❌ "Hope you're having a productive week!"

## Banned Style Habits
- ❌ Wall of text without spacing.
- ❌ Excessive emojis (e.g. 🚀🔥💡🎉 on every single line). Keep to 1-3 tasteful emojis max.
- ❌ Generic advice ("work hard", "stay consistent", "believe in yourself") without a concrete mechanism or story.
- ❌ Exceeding character limits by even 1 single character.
- ❌ Trailing ellipses (`...`) at the end of a truncated sentence. Posts must end on a complete thought.
