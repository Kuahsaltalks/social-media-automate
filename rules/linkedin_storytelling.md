# LinkedIn Storytelling Architecture

LinkedIn rewards authenticity combined with actionable takeaways. Never write bland corporate fluff. Write like a human talking to another human over coffee after a hard day.

## The 5-Beat Narrative Arc

1. **The Pattern Interrupt Hook (Lines 1–2)**:
   - Max 2 lines. Under 210 characters.
   - Sets up immediate stakes or a counter-intuitive observation.
   - Leaves an open loop that forces clicking "...see more".

2. **The Inciting Incident / Real-Life Experience (Context & Conflict)**:
   - Ground it in sensory, human details: a conversation, an awkward meeting, a failed launch, an unexpected realization.
   - Describe what was happening, what went wrong, or the friction faced.

3. **The Turning Point (The Shift)**:
   - The realization moment or breakthrough.
   - Why the old way failed and what clicked instead.

4. **The Distilled Playbook / Actionable Insight**:
   - Bullet points or clean short sentences outlining the lessons.
   - Clear takeaways the reader can apply today.

5. **The Low-Friction Closing Call to Action**:
   - Ask an engaging, non-generic question that invites opinions.
   - Avoid generic "Agree?" or "Thoughts?". Instead: "Have you ever faced [specific dilemma]?", "What would you have done differently?"

## LinkedIn Formatting Rules
- Use 1–2 sentence paragraphs with whitespace. Dense blocks of text are skipped on mobile screens.
- Avoid emoji spam. Maximum 2–3 tasteful emojis across the entire post.
- Never use buzzwords: "synergy", "paradigm shift", "pleased to announce", "game changer".
- Total length MUST NEVER exceed 3,000 characters.
