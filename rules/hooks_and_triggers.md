# Hook Engineering & Psychological Triggers

The hook determines 80% of content distribution. Readers scroll fast; you have less than 1.5 seconds to interrupt their scrolling pattern.

## 1. Core Psychological Levers

### A. The Contrarian / Ragebait Hook (Pattern Interrupt)
- Attack an accepted sacred cow or consensus belief.
- Highlight something everyone is doing wrong without realizing it.
- **Formulas**:
  - *"Stop doing [common activity]. It's quietly killing your [desirable outcome]."*
  - *"95% of people in [industry] think [common belief]. They are flat-out wrong."*
  - *"The worst advice I ever received was '[common phrase]'. Here is what happened when I did the opposite."*

### B. The Emotional / Relatable Real-Life Hook
- Start inside the middle of a high-stress, embarrassing, or vulnerable real-life moment.
- People don't connect with perfection; they connect with friction.
- **Formulas**:
  - *"Last Tuesday at 2 AM, I stared at a spreadsheet and realized I had wasted 6 months."*
  - *"I almost didn't post this because it makes me look foolish."*
  - *"My biggest career mistake didn't cost me money. It cost me 3 years."*

### C. The Curiosity Gap / Information Asymmetry Hook
- Create an open loop that the brain feels compelled to close.
- **Formulas**:
  - *"I spent 40 hours researching [topic]. Here is the 1 thing nobody is talking about."*
  - *"There are 2 types of [professionals]: those who understand [concept], and those who struggle."*
  - *"Most people think [X] causes [Y]. Science says it's actually [Z]."*

### D. The Direct Payoff Hook
- Quantifiable transformation or clear, immediate insight.
- **Formulas**:
  - *"How I turned [messy problem] into [clean solution] in 14 days (step-by-step):"*
  - *"If you have 3 minutes, this one mental shift will change how you think about [subject]."*

---

## 2. Platform-Specific Hook Constraints
- **X**: Hook is line 1. Must be under 90 characters so the rest of the tweet delivers the punchline.
- **LinkedIn**: The hook spans lines 1 and 2 (under 210 characters total) before the fold ("...see more"). Never waste line 1 on pleasantries like *"Hope you are having a great Monday!"*.
- **Threads**: Hook is conversational, feels like an unfiltered thought or a provocative shower-thought.
