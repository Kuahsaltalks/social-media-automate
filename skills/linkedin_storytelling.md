# Skill: LinkedIn Storytelling Crafting

## Objective
Transform raw dictation / research into a gripping, narrative-driven LinkedIn post strictly under 3,000 characters.

## Rules & Constraints
- HARD CEILING: 3,000 characters maximum. Target 1,500 - 2,500 characters.
- Never exceed 3,000 characters.
- Must follow the 5-Beat Narrative Arc:
  1. First 2 lines: High curiosity/tension hook (forces "...see more" click).
  2. Beat 2: Real-world human context/conflict (a specific situation or struggle people relate to).
  3. Beat 3: The pivot / moment of realization.
  4. Beat 4: Actionable lessons / framework (scannable bullet points).
  5. Beat 5: Emotion-resonant conclusion + specific discussion question.
- Format: Ample white space, short 1-2 sentence paragraphs for mobile readability.
