# Skill: Threads Post Crafting

## Objective
Craft a relatable, conversation-sparking micro-essay for Meta Threads strictly under 500 characters.

## Rules & Constraints
- HARD CEILING: 500 characters maximum. Target 400-470 chars.
- Never exceed 500 characters under any circumstance.
- Style: Raw, conversational, reflective, shower-thought energy. Less polished than LinkedIn, punchier than a blog.
- Structure:
  1. Relatable observation or contrarian question.
  2. Personal reflection or research nugget.
  3. Interactive closer inviting debate or shared experiences.
