# Skill: Carousel Slide Deck Builder

## Objective
Break down complex thoughts and research into a multi-slide visual carousel (5 to 8 slides).

## Branding & Identity (Carousel Specific)
- **Account Handle**: `@aiwithkaushal` (specifically used for all carousel decks instead of @kaushaltalks).

## Rules & Constraints
- Total Slides: Between 5 and 8 slides.
- Per-Slide Character Limit: Strict maximum 220 characters per slide.
- Format for each slide:
  - Slide Number & Title
  - Slide Copy (short, punchy, skimmable)
  - Visual Design Direction (guidance for background, graphics, or layout)
- Slide Flow:
  - Slide 1: Bold Hook Slide (stops the swipe, high visual contrast)
  - Slide 2: The Problem / The Common Mistake
  - Slide 3-N: The Step-by-Step Insights / Solutions (1 idea per slide)
  - **Final Slide (Mandatory CTA)**: Summary & Lead Magnet CTA:
    `Follow @aiwithkaushal and comment AI to join the free AI WhatsApp community.`
