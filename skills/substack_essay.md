# Skill: Substack Newsletter / Deep-Dive Essay

## Objective
Structure the research and dictation into a polished, high-value newsletter edition or deep-dive article.

## Structure
1. **Catchy Email Subject Line** (under 50 chars for high open rates)
2. **Preview Subtitle** (under 90 chars)
3. **The Hook / Narrative Opening** (draws the reader in with personal context or research paradox)
4. **The Core Thesis** (what makes this insight unique)
5. **The Deep Dive** (3 core sections with practical breakdowns)
6. **Summary & Actionable Playbook**
7. **Reader Discussion Prompt** (for comments section)
