# Skill: X (Twitter) Post Crafting

## Objective
Convert raw dictation / research thoughts into a single high-impact X post strictly under 280 characters.

## Rules & Constraints
- HARD CEILING: 280 characters maximum (including spaces and punctuation). Target 240-270 chars.
- Never exceed 280 characters under any circumstance.
- Structure:
  1. Opening Hook (First line - punchy, contrarian, or surprising)
  2. The Core Insight (1-2 sentences of high density value)
  3. The Takeaway or Open Question
- Style: Fast-paced, zero filler words (cut 'that', 'very', 'just', 'in order to').
- Hashtags: 0 or maximum 1 highly relevant hashtag.
- Formatting: Clean line breaks.
