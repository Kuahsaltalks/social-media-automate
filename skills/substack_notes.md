# Skill: Substack Notes Post Crafting

## Objective
Craft an intellectual, high-resonance micro-essay tailored for the Substack Notes feed strictly under 1,000 characters.

## Rules & Constraints
- HARD CEILING: 1,000 characters maximum. Target 400-750 characters.
- Audience: Readers, writers, thinkers, creators, and professionals who appreciate nuanced and philosophical ideas.
- Style: Grounded, authentic, reflective. Zero cheap hype or clickbait.
- Contraction Ban: Never use contractions. Always write out full words ("do not", "it is", "cannot", "I have").
- Structure:
  1. A sharp, philosophical or counter-intuitive opening statement.
  2. The underlying human or psychological reality.
  3. A lingering observation or reflective question for the community.
